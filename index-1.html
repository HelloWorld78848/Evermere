THE FIRST INKLINGS · CLASS VIII

Among Ruins and Stars

My philosophical side began to take shape when I was in Class 8. I was quite the history nerd back then — and, truth be told, I still am. I often found myself wondering what life was like in societies long gone. What did people think about as they watched the moon? Did they look up at the same stars and wonder what lay beyond them, just as we do today?

Different people, different timelines, but perhaps the same questions. For some reason, that thought always brought me comfort. It made the distance between us and those who lived centuries ago feel a little smaller.

The ruins that remain today were once someone's home, someone's entire world. They held someone's ideas, hopes, and purpose. Those lives have passed, and the places they inhabited now rest quietly — much like the people who once called them home. Yet something of them remains, and I find that thought strangely beautiful.
